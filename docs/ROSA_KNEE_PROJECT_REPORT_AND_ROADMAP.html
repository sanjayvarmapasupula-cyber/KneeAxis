1. Executive Overview & Mission

The ROSA-Knee Platform bridges patient CT/MRI volumetric scans and surgical execution on the Zimmer Biomet ROSA Knee Robotic System. Manual bone contouring and planning currently costs 45 to 90 minutes per surgical case with ±2.5 mm inter-observer variance. Our platform eliminates this bottleneck by performing multi-class 3D deep learning segmentation, anatomical axis extraction, and robotic cut planning in under 45 seconds with sub-millimeter geometric accuracy.

2. Completed Milestones (What Has Been Built Till Now)

Multi-Planar Radiological Viewer

Axial, coronal, sagittal viewports with crosshair sync, HU windowing (Bone/Soft Tissue), and 19-point volumetric pre-flight QA check.

3D nnU-Net Deep Learning

6-class semantic segmentation trained on the OAI-ZIB knee dataset. Isolates Femur, Tibia, Patella, and femoral/tibial articular cartilage.

Interactive 3D WebGL Mesh Viewer

High-framerate Three.js WebGL canvas displaying patient bone meshes, Mechanical Axis, TEA, Whiteside's Line, and dynamic resection planes.

Robotic Pre-Op Resection Calculator

Calculates distal femoral cut depth/valgus and proximal tibial cut depth/posterior slope with automatic Zimmer Biomet implant sizing.

Surgical Dossier & QA Sign-Off

Automated generation of formal surgical dossiers, joint alignment summaries, digital radiologist approval, and printable records.

Resilient Dual-Engine Backend

Offline high-fidelity demonstration engine with live failover connection to Python FastAPI / PyTorch deep learning service.

4. What We Are Going To Do (Phased Roadmap)

Implement dynamic WebGL plane-clipping shaders so surgeons can visually inspect simulated bone resections in real-time as millimetric depths and angles are adjusted.

Sprint 2: Direct ROSA Robotic Protocol XML/JSON Export Month 2

Generate native XML surgical plan files formatted for direct network upload or USB loading into the Zimmer Biomet ROSA Knee surgical console.

Sprint 3: Ligament Laxity & Flexion-Extension Gap Balancing Month 3

Integrate mathematical gap balancing curve modeling joint space tension at 0°, 45°, 90°, and 120° flexion to prevent post-op joint tightness or instability.

Sprint 4: Live Intra-Op Optical Tracker Calibration Stream Months 4-6
