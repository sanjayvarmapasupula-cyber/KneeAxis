# Legal and Privacy Page Verification Checklist

The included Privacy Policy and Terms of Use are **draft templates**, not authoritative legal notices. Verify every point before publishing them, especially if real identifiable health data is used.

- [ ] Official operator/team/college entity name and address
- [ ] Responsible privacy/contact email, actually monitored
- [ ] Policy effective date, change-notification procedure
- [ ] Who can create accounts, actual role and access rules
- [ ] Which categories of patient, image, account, telemetry and derived data are collected
- [ ] Documented consent, research approvals or other legitimate basis for processing
- [ ] Intended data uses, including whether datasets are used for model training
- [ ] Hosting and server geography, including intranet, third parties, backups and transfers
- [ ] Actual security practices (access control, encryption, audit logging) and limitations
- [ ] Actual data retention periods and deletion processes
- [ ] All processors/service providers and whether third-party AI APIs are used
- [ ] Account and privacy-rights request mechanism
- [ ] Cookie/session/analytics tools actually used; preference controls if legally required
- [ ] Incident-response contact process
- [ ] Ownership/licensing of research datasets and model outputs
- [ ] Intellectual property, acceptable use, limitations and governing-law provisions
- [ ] Clearly stated research-only/non-clinical deployment limitation
- [ ] Legal review relevant to jurisdiction and institution before public release

**Do not claim HIPAA/GDPR/DPDP compliance, certification, encryption-at-rest, external approvals, or a formal clinical validation program unless established and documented.**
